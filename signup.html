Sign up for a shift

Pick any shifts in the next two weeks, then add your name and Northwestern email. No experience needed. Wear long pants and closed-toe shoes; we'll give you gloves and a hairnet.

Shifts are at our kitchen at 1835 Hinman Avenue: how to find it.

What happens on each shift?
Recovery
Pick up surplus food from our partners and bring it back to the kitchen.
Resourcing
Sort, weigh, temp and label what came in.
Meal prep
Portion the food into meals and label them.
Delivery
Take the meals to the community fridges around Evanston.
